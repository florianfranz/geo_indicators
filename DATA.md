# Data Documentation

## ETOPO Global Relief Model (Ice Surface)

The original data is from the ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model provided by NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information.

**Citation:**
NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. 2022: ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74

**Modifications:**

- Tiles were downloaded separately and merged into a single, global raster file 
- The global raster was resampled to a 0.1° x 0.1° resolution

## Palaeogeographic maps

We provide palaeogeographic maps that are derived from the PANALESIS plate tectonic model. These maps have a 10x10km resolution and are given in equal area projection. They are already corrected for sea-level variations.
We provide reconstructions covering the entire Phanerozoic for PANALESIS v0 (000 - 545 Ma), and from 330 to 550 for PANALESIS v1 (still work in progress).

The data is also accessible [on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15396264).

**Citation:**
Franziskakis, F., Vérard, C., Castelltort, S., & Giuliani, G. (2025). Global Quantified Palaeogeographic Maps and Associated Sea-level Variations for the Phanerozoic using the PANALESIS Model [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15396265
